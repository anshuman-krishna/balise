# NNNN. Title, as a statement of the decision

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Area**: measurement | statistics | ledger | criteria | platform | interface

## Context

What was true when this came up. The forces, the constraints, the thing that
went wrong or was about to. Written so that someone reading it in a year, who
was not here, understands the problem before they see the answer.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense and as an instruction. "The band carries
the energy models", not "we decided to make the band carry the energy models".

## Consequences

What follows, including what it costs. If the decision makes something harder,
say which thing and how much harder. If it forecloses an option, name the
option.

## Alternatives considered

Each one with the reason it was not taken. An alternative dismissed in half a
sentence was not really considered.
